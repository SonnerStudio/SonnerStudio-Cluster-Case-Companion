# Code of Conduct — SonnerStudio Cluster-Case-Companion (SS-CCC)

## Our Pledge

Contributors, researchers, and **Internet WAN Cluster / Audio-Party** participants agree to foster an open, welcoming, and harassment-free environment across GitHub Issues, Pull Requests, Discussions, Wiki edits, and live cluster rooms.

## Expected Behavior

- **Respectful Collaboration:** Give constructive, evidence-based technical feedback on benchmarks, sharding algorithms, SwiftUI views, and translations.
- **Reproducible Science:** Publish honest, unmanipulated hardware, `TTFT`/`TPOT`, energy (`J/token`), and RDMA/WAN metrics when participating in **Science & Roofline** benchmarks or Record Challenges.
- **Audio-Party & WAN Etiquette:** Respect voice channels (`/ws/wan-audio`), avoid disruptive audio flooding, and never share private room codes or SSH keys publicly.
- **Inclusive Localization:** Welcome contributors helping improve all **17 supported languages**.

## Enforcement

Maintainers reserve the right to remove abusive comments, revoke WAN coordinator room tokens, or restrict repository access for conduct that harms community safety or research integrity.
